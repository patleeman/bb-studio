# Duo v7: edge-to-edge surfaces, safe fixed controls

Unfolded Duo and the shared iPad regular layout paint the rail, sidebars, detail
panes and right-page header backgrounds to the window edges. The rail has one
material layer across its full height; overlapping materials previously made
the home-indicator strip look like a different band.

Transcript and Studio content use the full right-page width, including the
status-bar column. Their surrounding layout still positions fixed header
buttons, composer, quick replies and jump button inside the window safe area.
Native scroll views already cover the bottom edge; their existing content
insets keep the final row clear of the home indicator and fixed bottom bars.
The composer retains its home-indicator inset. The narrow and portrait paths
keep their existing layout. No scroll anchoring or keyboard logic changed.

`DuoEdgeToEdgeUITests` checks the active detail/background bounds, header
background width, native scroll bounds and bottom insets, fixed control frames,
and the jump button in mid-history. Each capture also compares actual rail
pixels at the middle and bottom, so a material seam cannot pass on geometry
alone. The test runs on iPhone and iPad idioms at 951×669.

Fixtures: an isolated staged server with Pages, Mini apps and a folder; a long
Studio page; and the local Bot/thread fixture with 600 mixed-height messages.
Notes and voice capabilities are enabled in the captures. No agents ran.

Measured Duo bounds: scrolling content and detail/header backgrounds reach
x=951; the rail background reaches y=669. The Bot composer ends at x=855 and
y=627, inside the 84-point trailing and 34-point bottom system safe areas,
with their existing inner padding. Rail center/bottom RGB samples differ by
one level or less in all three renders.

Validation passed: 341 iOS unit tests (one skip), edge/pixel/control UI tests on
Duo and iPad (including native iPad landscape), keyboard and outer/portrait
chrome checks, both long-transcript fold-retention tests, quick-reply clipping
and fade checks, iPad sidebar alignment, Studio typecheck, 1,982 Vitest tests,
and icon/documentation/whitespace checks. No TestFlight build or upload ran.
