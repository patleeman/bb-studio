# Duo v8: design review and polish

A fresh review of the unfolded layout (Bot, Threads, Studio, Settings) at
951×669 pt, iPhone idiom, in dark and light, against the iPad layout at the same
size. It builds on [v7](V7.md), whose edge-to-edge surfaces and safe controls
were kept. The folded phone layout and portrait didn't change.

## What changed

- **Sidebar rows reach the bottom edge.** The v7 sidebar list already extended to
  y=669 in UIKit, but its SwiftUI `.clipped()` frame stopped at the home
  indicator. Rows were cut off on a hard line 34 pt above the bottom, leaving a
  black band under the left page. The sidebar now clips only its left and right
  edges. Its native bottom inset still lets the last row scroll clear of the
  indicator.
- **The crease line runs the full height.** It used to stop at the home
  indicator too.
- **One sidebar surface.** The sidebar headers (title, actions and search) were a
  grey `.bar` band over a black list, unlike the desktop and iPad sidebars. They
  now share the sidebar's grouped background, so header, search, list and crease
  read as one page. The right-page header keeps its bar.
- **Whole rounded selection.** A selected sidebar row now has a complete
  12-point rounded highlight. The list used to clip the fill to the section's
  shape, so a highlighted first row had rounded top corners and a square bottom.
- **Studio's All items shows its state.** All items used the list's default grey
  cell while the other folders were clear. It is now highlighted when it is the
  active folder, and folders report `isSelected` to accessibility.
- **Matching row insets.** Row content had 20 pt of padding on the leading side
  but only 4 pt on the trailing side on the Duo (16 pt on iPad), so timestamps
  nearly touched the highlight. Both sides now have 20 pt.
- **Tighter Threads sidebar.** The open Bot thread, Automations and the thread
  list are headerless sections. They sat 35 pt apart and now use compact section
  spacing, as Studio already did.
- **Settings cards keep a margin.** v7 let the Settings form run under the
  status column, so its cards, toggle and stepper touched the window's right
  edge. The grouped background still goes edge to edge, but the cards end 20 pt
  inside the safe edge, matching their leading margin.
- **iPad status bar.** At the shared 951×669 size, the iPad window has a 32 pt
  top inset. The rail, sidebar and header backgrounds now fill it instead of
  leaving a black strip.
- **Studio filter pills in light mode.** Unselected pills used the secondary
  background, which matches the grouped sidebar in light mode, so Pages and Mini
  apps had no visible pill. They now use the search field's fill on the sidebar.

Kept as they were: the transcript and Studio content running under the status
column (v7), the quick-reply fade, the Bot's floating composer, the regular
thread composer's bar, and `TranscriptResizeAnchor`.

## Tests

`DuoEdgeToEdgeUITests` now writes these captures and also checks:

- the crease hairline reaches y=669 at the window midpoint;
- a Bot sidebar card, not the bare sidebar, shows at the bottom edge when its
  list overflows (pixel sample);
- the sidebar header pixel matches the sidebar body (no separate band);
- the rail, header and sidebar header start at y=0 (fills iPad's status bar);
- Studio shows its pills and Folders, and All items is selected;
- a Studio row's timestamp ends 20 pt inside its card, like the leading inset;
- Settings has no header actions, and its toggle and server field end at least
  20 pt inside the safe edge.

Set `BBGO_QA_LIGHT=1` to run the same checks in light mode.

## Validation

- `BBStudioTests` on the owned iPhone Duo: 341 tests, 1 skipped, 0 failures.

  (folded and portrait chrome), `DuoLongTranscriptUITests` (fold retention),
  `DuoQuickRepliesUITests`, `DuoRegularComparisonUITests` and
  `DuoSidebarSearchUITests` passed.
- On the iPad Pro 13-inch: `DuoEdgeToEdgeUITests` (including native iPad
  landscape), `DuoLayoutUITests` (window-size retention and keyboard),
  `DuoRegularComparisonUITests` and `DuoSidebarSearchUITests` passed.

  `DuoLayoutUITests` likewise targets the native iPad window.
