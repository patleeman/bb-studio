Ask for a plan, get a real doc.

Pages are documents with charts, stats, tables and live embeds. You and the agent edit the same page at the same time, and it stays linked to the chat that made it.

Describe it. Click through it.

Designs are live screens and clickable prototypes. Play them, comment on any element, and get the next round.

Need a tracker? Ask.

Mini apps, live dashboards and saved artifacts, built on the spot and kept for next time. They run beside the chat, in a page or in their own tab.

One Bot. Many conversations. One memory.

Workers take the long jobs, approvals come to you, and replies thread like Slack, right under the message.

Everything side by side.

Docs, apps and code open in tabs beside the conversation. Split them, or find anything with ⌘K.

Talk instead of type.

Dictate into any message, page or comment: text appears as you speak, and the audio is saved as you go. Record a meeting and turn it into notes with decisions and to-dos.

Transcription uses the voice service you choose in Settings. Recorded calls can be transcribed on your Mac.

It remembers. It learns your routines. It runs them for you.

Memory keeps what matters, Skills capture how you like things done, and Automations and Todos bring the work back at the right time.

Code, with the agent pointing.

VS Code opens beside the chat, and the agent can show you exactly which lines it means.

Sketch it together.

Whiteboards live inside your pages, and you and the agent draw on the same one.

Find anything you've made.

The Library keeps every page, design, whiteboard and app in one place, searchable down to the content.

All your accounts, one glance.

See every sign-in, its limits and what is running on it. When one runs low, switch with one click.
