BB Studio

A desktop app where you work with AI agents through your Bots.

Each Bot is a space of its own: a Main conversation, parallel conversations, the workers it runs, and its own memory, library, todos, automations and skills. You talk to the Bot. The Bot runs the work and comes back to you when it needs you.

A very opinionated fork of the excellent bb, by Michael Yong. MIT License. Download

The philosophy

bb is an excellent open source app for running AI coding agents, by Michael Yong, under the MIT License. It runs agent threads from Claude Code, Codex, Pi and other providers on your own machines, organized by project.

BB Studio is a very opinionated fork of it. It makes one choice and builds the app around it: you work through Bots. A Bot has a name, a look, a memory and its own pages, and every conversation, worker and file belongs to one. The Studio plugins come built in, so pages, whiteboards, designs, dashboards and mini apps are part of the app.

The fork stays thin: bb's core is kept, its license file is unchanged, and BB Studio's changes are replayed onto bb's latest code as bb moves on.

You work with your Bots

You work with your Bots, not with a list of threads. It is a collaboration: you set the direction and make the calls, the Bot does the work, and you can see what it is doing at any time.

  • Main. Each Bot has a Main conversation. Reports and alerts arrive there.
  • Needs you. A conversation that waits on you is outlined in amber, with the reason on its second line. The Bot's icon in the switcher gets an amber mark.
  • Awareness. Each conversation knows what the Bot's other conversations and workers are doing.
  • Several Bots. The switcher at the bottom of the sidebar moves between them. Each keeps its own theme, presence and pages.

Bot spaces

A Bot's space holds its conversations and its workers, side by side in the sidebar. Open as many conversations with a Bot as you need. Each has its own transcript and model, and all of them share the Bot's memory, skills and todos.

Workers

A Bot starts workers for longer jobs. A worker opens read-only, with its state in the header. In place of a composer it has one box: Ask the Bot about this worker. Your question goes to the Bot, in the conversation that owns the worker.

Approvals

When a worker needs an approval, the card shows in the conversation that owns it, not in the worker. It names the worker and shows the command and its folder. Deny, approve once, or approve for that worker.

Threads and forks

Point at a Bot's reply and choose Reply in thread. The thread opens in the side panel, beside the conversation. Under the message, a row shows how many replies the thread has and when the last one came. A fork of a message becomes a conversation of its own, at the same level as the others, with a link to where it came from.

The workbench

Documents open beside the conversation, in a side panel with up to two panes. Links, cards in replies, search results and the Bot itself open things there.

Classic

A Classic theme is a solid palette with no scene. The eight use published palettes: bb Default, One Dark, Dracula, Solarized Light, GitHub Light, GitHub Dark, Nord and Catppuccin Mocha.

{{theme theme-github-light "GitHub Light" ""}} {{theme theme-catppuccin-mocha "Catppuccin Mocha" ""}}

Every theme passes contrast and frame-time checks before it is saved. The scene pauses when the window is not in front, holds still under reduced motion, and renders smaller on battery. Bots can make new themes with the theme tools.

Presence

A Bot's icon is a live form drawn in its theme's colors. The form shows what the Bot is doing:

  • Idle. Slow breathing.
  • Thinking. Faster, with a shimmer, while a turn runs.
  • Working. One orbiting satellite for each running worker.
  • Needs you. Amber, with two pulse rings.

There are eight forms: orb, tesseract, torus knot, particle swarm, Möbius ribbon, polyhedron, Lorenz attractor and Klein bottle. A Bot can also write its own form. Custom forms run in a sandbox with no network.

Edit space

Edit space sets a Bot's name, presence, theme and default model in one view. Previews run live as you choose.

Bot pages

Every Bot has the same pages. Memory, Library, Automations and Todos are one click from its sidebar; Skills is in the Bot's controls. A row on any page opens its item in the workbench.

The Archive page holds older and finished conversations and older workers. A finished conversation opens read-only, with Reopen.

Accounts

Accounts, in the sidebar footer, lists every sign-in your agents run on, in one place.

  • Status. Each account shows Ready, Signed out, Error, Limited or Off.
  • Limits. Its 5-hour and 7-day windows and per-model limits, with reset times.
  • Threads. The threads that ran on that account in the last hour.
  • Sign in. Sign in again from the account's row.

When an account signs out or a window passes 90%, a small card says so, with one button: Sign in, Use other account or Switch model. Tokens stay with the account pooler; nothing in the app shows them.

iPhone

An iPhone app is in progress and in testing through TestFlight. It follows the same model: your Bots, their Main, conversations and workers, approvals and notifications from the phone, and Studio pages. It is not available to the public yet.
