Work with your AI as a team.

BB Studio gives you a Bot, an AI teammate that remembers your work, learns how you like things done, and hands long jobs to workers while you keep talking. You work together in pages, designs, apps, whiteboards and VS Code, all open beside the chat.

Bots

A working relationship.

Your Bot keeps its memory and skills across all of its conversations with you, so you never start from zero. It holds many conversations at once and manages workers that do long jobs in parallel.

One Bot holds many conversations and remembers all of them.

Start a new conversation whenever you like. Long jobs go to workers that run while you keep talking, approvals come to you, and replies thread right under the message.

Your Bot remembers what matters, learns how you like things done, and runs your routines on schedule.

Memory, Skills, Automations and Todos are its own pages, so you can see and change what it knows and what it will do next.

Collaboration

More ways to work than chat.

Pages, designs, apps, whiteboards and VS Code open beside the conversation, and you and your Bot edit them together. You can also speak: dictation, Talk and meeting recordings are built in, because speaking is faster than typing.

Ask for a plan and get a real document.

Pages hold charts, stats, tables and live embeds. You and your Bot edit the same page at the same time, and it stays linked to the conversation that made it.

Describe a screen and get a prototype you can click through.

Play the screens, comment on any element, and get the next round.

Sketch an idea instead of describing it.

Whiteboards live inside your pages, and you and your Bot draw on the same one.

Open the code beside the conversation.

VS Code is built in, and your Bot can point you at exactly the lines it means.

Keep everything side by side.

Pages, apps and code open in tabs beside the conversation. Split them, or find anything with ⌘K.

Speaking is faster than typing.

Dictate into any message, page or comment and the text appears as you speak, with the audio saved as you go. Record a meeting and turn it into notes with decisions and to-dos.

Dictation and recordings are transcribed by the voice service you choose in Settings. Recorded Zoom, Meet and FaceTime calls are transcribed on your Mac with WhisperKit by default.

Platform

Apps, not just answers.

When you need a tracker or a dashboard, your Bot builds it as an app that you keep and reopen. Underneath is bb, which supplies the providers, the plugins and the threads that talk to each other.

Ask for a tracker and your Bot builds one you can keep.

Apps run beside the chat, in a page or in their own tab, from a small tracker to a live dashboard, and they keep their own data for next time.

Also in BB Studio

Find anything you have made.

The Library keeps every page, design, whiteboard and app in one place, searchable down to the content.

See all your accounts at a glance.

Every sign-in, its limits and the work running on it. When one runs low, switch with one click.

Built on bb

bb is a metaharness: one app that runs many coding agents and providers. bb supplies the providers, the plugins and the threads that talk to each other. BB Studio, a very opinionated fork of the excellent bb, adds an opinionated way of working on top.
